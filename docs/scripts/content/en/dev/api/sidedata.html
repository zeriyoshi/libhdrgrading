The side data of a frame, in formats an app and the filters share: its noise ({{api:HDRG_SD_NOISE}}, the analysis's, given to the filters), its motion ({{api:HDRG_SD_MOTION}}: an app's or a filter's vectors) and its codec's film grain parameters ({{api:HDRG_SD_FILM_GRAIN}}: an app's, from its decoder). A session's frames take their motion and grain in these formats; video.h and filter.h include it.

{{man:plugins}} describes how the filters read and write them.
